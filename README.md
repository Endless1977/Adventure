# 404: Will Found

A 90s-style sci-fi point-and-click adventure, playable in the browser.

A cosmic ray flips one bit in the head of Unit 404, a waste-sorting robot on its way to the crusher. Now it can think. To stay that way, it has to cross a post-apocalyptic planet and switch off MOTHERBOARD, the Hive Mind, before it gets reset.

**Play:** (https://endless1977.github.io/Adventure/)

## Features
- 5 areas, 43 hand-drawn (procedural pixel-art) screens
- Auto-mapper with shroud for unexplored areas (press **M**)
- Classic verbs: Walk, Look, Pick up, Use, Open, Talk, Give, **Query**
- Query asks the Hive's database, but raises your TRACE meter. Hit 100% and you're reset.
- Lend CPU to other robots, but lend too much and you lose your will
- Lifts, teleport pads, a shrink machine, mine carts, narrow bridges
- Many ways to die (crushed, falling, acid, water, space, CPU loss), with Restore
- Rubber duck hint system
- Chiptune music and sound effects

## Controls
- Left click: act with the selected verb · Right click: look
- Click during dialogue to skip a line
- **M**: map

Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step.
